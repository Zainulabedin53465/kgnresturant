The Mandi Tradition

A feast made to be shared

Mandi is a celebrated dish of Arabian origin: long-grain rice, gently spiced and cooked slowly with tender meat until every grain carries the flavour. It is traditionally served on one large platter, made for the whole table to gather around.

KGN Mandi House brings that experience to Ghatikia, Bhubaneswar. Come with family and friends, and share a platter of Mandi.

  • Fragrant Rice

    Aromatic rice, slow-cooked and layered with warm spices.

  • Tender Chicken

    Grilled chicken that comes off the bone with ease.

  • Made to Share

    Big platters and good company, the way Mandi is meant to be enjoyed.
